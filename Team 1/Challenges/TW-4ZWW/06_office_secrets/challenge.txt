06 - Office Secrets
Category: Forensics   Level: Easy   Points: 100
------------------------------------------------------------
The finance team sent this budget spreadsheet to everyone in the company.
They say it "only contains the public numbers". Companies leak secrets this
way more often than you would think.

File: budget_2026.xlsx  (Excel, LibreOffice Calc or Google Sheets)

Goal: find the flag that should never have been sent.
