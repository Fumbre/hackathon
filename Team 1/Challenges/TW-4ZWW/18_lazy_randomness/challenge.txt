18 - Lazy Randomness
Category: Crypto   Level: Very hard   Points: 300
------------------------------------------------------------
A cheap brand of (made-up) smart routers makes its own RSA key the first
time it boots. Its random number generator is so bad that some routers
end up with keys that have a prime number in common.

We collected the public keys of four routers and one encrypted message
that was sent to router C. RSA keys with 2048 bits are far too big to
factor... normally.

Files: keys/router_A.pem ... keys/router_D.pem   (RSA public keys)
       message_for_C.txt                         (the encrypted message)

Goal: break router C's key and decrypt the message.
(The message was encrypted with "textbook" RSA: c = m^e mod n, no padding.)
