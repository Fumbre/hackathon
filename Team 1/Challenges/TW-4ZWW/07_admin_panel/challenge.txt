07 - Admin Panel
Category: Web   Level: Medium   Points: 150
------------------------------------------------------------
Hunebed Hosting built a "secure" admin panel for its staff. You only have
the guest account that was printed on a sticky note:

    username: guest
    password: guest

The admin area is locked for guests. The developer says the session is
"encrypted, so nobody can change it". Is that true?

File: panel/index.html  (open it in your browser, it works offline)

Goal: get into the admin area and read the flag.
