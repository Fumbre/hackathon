10 - Old Database
Category: Data   Level: Medium   Points: 150
------------------------------------------------------------
Hunebed Hosting moved to a new system and left its old helpdesk database
on a shared drive. The new admin claims "there is nothing sensitive left
in it". Read the tickets: somebody moved something they should not have.

File: helpdesk.db  (an SQLite database)

Free viewer that works on Windows, Mac and Linux: DB Browser for SQLite
(sqlitebrowser.org). Use "Browse Data" to look around and "Execute SQL"
to run queries.

Goal: find the flag. You will need at least one small SQL query.
