09 - Sensor Log
Category: Data   Level: Medium   Points: 150
------------------------------------------------------------
The campus has wireless temperature sensors that send their readings over a
slow LoRa radio network. Readings often arrive late and out of order.

Yesterday sensor TH-13 (server room) was switched into calibration mode
(mode CAL). In that mode it sends ONE character per reading in the "diag"
column. Read those characters in the order they were MEASURED (not the
order they were received) and you get a message.

File: sensor_log.csv  (Excel, LibreOffice Calc or Google Sheets)

Goal: rebuild the message from TH-13 and find the flag.

Tip for Excel with Dutch settings: if everything ends up in column A, use
Data -> From Text/CSV and choose "Comma" as the delimiter.
