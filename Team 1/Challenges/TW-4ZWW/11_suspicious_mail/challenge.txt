11 - Suspicious Mail
Category: Forensics   Level: Medium   Points: 150
------------------------------------------------------------
An employee of Hunebed Hosting (a made-up company) forwarded this email to
the security team: "Is this real? It looks like it comes from our IT
department." It is a training example: every address and link in it is
fake (they end in .invalid, which can never exist on the internet).

File: urgent_password_expiry.eml  (open it in Outlook, Apple Mail or
Thunderbird, or just in a text editor - an .eml file is plain text)

Goal: find out who really sent it and where the button REALLY points to.
The flag is hidden in that real link.
