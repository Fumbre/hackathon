16 - Repeating Key
Category: Crypto   Level: Hard   Points: 250
------------------------------------------------------------
The radio club intercepted an encrypted message between two (made-up)
smugglers. Their encryption program is simple: it XORs every byte of the
English message with a password that is repeated over and over. The
password is somewhere between 6 and 16 letters long. We do not know it.

Files: intercepted.bin   (the raw encrypted bytes)
       intercepted.hex   (the same bytes written as hex text, handy for
                          CyberChef or Python)

Goal: break the encryption without knowing the password and find the flag
inside the message.
